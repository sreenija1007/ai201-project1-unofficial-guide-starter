4 stars - Late night is a lifesaver
De Neve Late Night is the best thing about this dining hall. After 10pm when you have been studying for hours and everything else is closed, being able to walk in and get real food is amazing. The late night menu is solid, not just snacks. Runs until about 2am on weeknights which is perfect for finals season.

3 stars - Decent but crowded
The food at De Neve is fine. It is classic American dining hall food, burgers, pizza, fried chicken, chili. Nothing fancy but consistent and filling. The problem is it is always crowded at dinner. Lines regularly hit 20-30 minutes at peak time. If you can handle the crowds the variety is pretty good.

5 stars - Best variety on campus
De Neve has the most options of any dining hall. You can get American comfort food, pizza, salad bar, vegetarian options all in one place. It is all you can eat so you can go back as many times as you want. For people who eat a lot or like variety this is the best spot on The Hill.

4 stars - Go for late night, skip peak dinner
The trick with De Neve is timing. Peak dinner 6-7pm is a nightmare. Go at 5pm or use late night after 10pm and it is a totally different experience. The food quality is consistent and there is always something you want to eat. The flex bar with salad and fruit is always stocked.

2 stars - Too crowded, food is average
I know De Neve is popular but I never understood why. The food is average dining hall food and the lines make it not worth it most of the time. Bruin Plate has better quality and Epicuria is less crowded. De Neve is fine if you need something quick and filling but it is not exciting.

5 stars - Late night during finals
There is no better place on campus during finals week than De Neve Late Night. Open until 2am, real food options, and full of other students also studying. It becomes a social spot as much as a dining hall. The meal plan access at night is a huge perk and one of the best reasons to have a meal plan.

4 stars - Classic and reliable
De Neve is the old reliable of UCLA dining. You always know what you are getting, American comfort food, pizza, salad, desserts. It is not the most exciting but it is always there and always open the latest. The Late Night option makes it essential especially in the second half of the quarter when things get stressful.
