UCLA Dining provides a range of dining options for students, faculty, staff, and guests. During the academic school year, we offer four all-you-care-to-eat residential restaurants located in Carnesale Commons, Sunset Village, Rieber Court, and De Neve Plaza. Additionally, our quick-service locations include Bruin Cafe, Cafe 1919, The Drey, The Study at Hedrick, Rendezvous, Bruin Bowl and Epicuria at Ackerman, giving you convenient choices across campus.

Each of the four Residential Restaurants serves as many as ten entree options at every meal. All meals are buffet style and all-you-care-to-eat. Pay by Meal Plan, BruinCard Easy Pay, or Meal Ticket only. Cash or credit cards are not accepted at the restaurants.

Bruin Plate
A leader in healthy, sustainable and mindful eating. Located in Sunset Village. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily during the academic year.

Epicuria at Covel
Simple, delicious Mediterranean meals. Located in Covel Commons. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily.

De Neve Dining
Traditional American dining with a wide variety of options. Located in De Neve Plaza. Offers De Neve Late Night service after regular dinner hours. Open until 2am on weekdays for students with meal plans.

Feast at Rieber
International cuisine and rotating themed meals. Located in Rieber Hall. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Meal Plan Information
Students living in the residence halls are required to purchase a meal plan. Optional meal plans are available for students living off campus or in university apartments. Meal plans include a set number of swipes per week plus dining dollars for use at quick-service locations. Guests can purchase meal tickets at the door for residential restaurants during breakfast and lunch. Dinner at residential restaurants is restricted to meal plan holders only.
