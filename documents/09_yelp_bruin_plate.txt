5 stars - Best campus dining hall I have been to
Bruin Plate is genuinely one of the best dining experiences I have had at any university. The food is fresh, healthy, and actually tastes good. They source locally which you can taste in the quality. The tuna poke bowl is incredible and the roasted vegetable options are always well seasoned. No soda here which I appreciate. The fruit water is a nice touch. Lines can be long around 6pm but if you go at 5pm or after 7 it is much more manageable.

4 stars - Great food, annoying lines
The food quality is legitimately great for a dining hall. Bruin Plate lives up to the hype. My only issue is the lines during peak dinner time which can take 20-30 minutes. Go early and it is a completely different experience. The themed days like garlic day are fun and creative. Vegetarian and vegan options are the best on campus by far.

5 stars - My go-to every day
I eat at Bruin Plate almost every day. The menu rotates enough to stay interesting and the quality is consistent. Healthy food that does not taste like health food. The walk from the lower dorms is worth it every single time. Staff is friendly and the dining room is nicer than most dining halls.

3 stars - Good but overrated
Do not get me wrong the food is good. But people talk about it like it is a restaurant and it is still a dining hall. The portions are a bit smaller than De Neve and the healthy options mean less comfort food. If you want a burger or pizza at midnight go to De Neve. If you want to feel good about what you ate go to Bruin Plate.

4 stars - Best option for vegetarians
As a vegetarian Bruin Plate is the only dining hall where I feel like I have real choices. The plant-based options are creative and filling not just a sad salad. The grain bowls and flatbreads are excellent. Highly recommend for anyone with dietary restrictions.

5 stars - Hidden gem timing trick
Go at 5:15pm. No line, full food options, and you can take your time. The people who complain about Bruin Plate lines just go at the wrong time. The food itself is excellent across the board. Arugula flatbread and the roasted herb chicken are my favorites.

4 stars - Great for health conscious students
Really impressed by how seriously they take healthy eating here. Local produce, organic options, no processed junk. The dining hall itself feels cleaner and brighter than De Neve. Only giving 4 stars because it closes earlier which is annoying.
