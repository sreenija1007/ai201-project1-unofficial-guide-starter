UCLA Dining has been ranked number 1 Best College Food in America by Niche.com for the seventh consecutive year. This is the ninth time in the past ten years that UCLA has claimed the top spot. The rankings are primarily based on student surveys with meal plan affordability also factored in.

UCLA Dining offers a vibrant mix of nearly a dozen quick-service restaurants and all-you-care-to-eat dining halls. Each location is designed with atmosphere, comfort and community in mind.

Bruin Plate emphasizes healthy eating with locally sourced produce and meats, sustainable seafood, organic options, and a wide array of vegetarian and vegan dishes. Communal tables and a private dining room foster connection. Bruin Plate does not serve soda. Instead it offers fruit-infused water such as pineapple, lime, and strawberry. The hall hosts themed food days where all dishes incorporate one ingredient such as garlic day or strawberry day.

Epicuria at Covel and Epicuria at Ackerman are dedicated to Mediterranean menus and flavors. Fresh pasta bar with choice of two sauces, pizza section, and seasonal Mediterranean dishes. Simple cooking methods and fresh ingredients define the menu.

De Neve Dining serves a variety of North, South and Central American food ranging from chili and midwest pepper steak to burgers, fried chicken, pizza and more. There is also a flex bar with salad bar, fruits and vegetarian options. De Neve is known as the traditional dining hall with classic American dishes. De Neve Late Night offers extended hours after dinner service.

The Study at Hedrick blends dining with academics. Students enjoy an artisan bakery, a coffee and tea bar, a 24-hour study space, private meeting rooms, free Wi-Fi, and cozy seating areas.

Rendezvous serves Asian and Latin options including sushi, tacos, and rice bowls.

Bruin Bowl returns this fall with healthy custom-made bowls for guests on the go.

UCLA Dining serves more than 15000 on-campus residents daily. The program sources ingredients from local Southern California farms and works to reduce food waste. Kosher and halal options are available at select locations. Students with food allergies are encouraged to meet with the registered dietician.
