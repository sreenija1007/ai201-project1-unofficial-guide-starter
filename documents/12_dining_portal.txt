UCLA Dining Portal

UCLA Dining provides a range of dining options for students, faculty, staff, and guests. During the academic school year we offer four all-you-care-to-eat residential restaurants and multiple quick-service locations.

Residential Restaurants (All-You-Care-To-Eat)
Bruin Plate - Sunset Village - Healthy and sustainable eating, locally sourced ingredients, no soda served, fruit-infused water available, vegetarian and vegan focused
Epicuria at Covel - Covel Commons - Mediterranean cuisine, fresh pasta bar, pizza, simple cooking methods
De Neve Dining - De Neve Plaza - American and international variety, late night service available, flex bar with salad and vegetarian options
Feast at Rieber - Rieber Hall - International rotating menu, themed cuisine nights

Quick Service Locations
Bruin Cafe - Coffee, pastries, grab and go
The Study at Hedrick - Artisan bakery, coffee bar, open 24 hours, study space
Rendezvous - Asian and Latin cuisine
Epicuria at Ackerman - Mediterranean, located in Ackerman Union
Bruin Bowl - Custom bowls, healthy options
The Drey - Sandwiches, comfort food, salads
Cafe 1919 - Coffee and light bites

Meal Plan Information
Residential students are required to purchase a meal plan. Optional plans available for non-residential students. Meal plan dollars can be used at all dining locations. Meal swipes for residential restaurants are available at breakfast and lunch for all meal plan holders. Dinner at residential restaurants is restricted to students living in the residence halls with active meal plans. Guests may purchase meal tickets at the door for breakfast and lunch only. Cash and credit cards not accepted at residential restaurants.

Allergen and Dietary Information
UCLA Dining offers allergen-friendly options at all locations. A registered dietician is available for consultations. Kosher certified options available at select locations. Halal options available. Vegan and vegetarian options clearly labeled at all locations.

Meal Plan Pricing
Prices vary by plan type and are updated each academic year. Contact UCLA Housing for current pricing. Plans range from limited swipe options to unlimited access packages with varying dining dollar amounts included.
