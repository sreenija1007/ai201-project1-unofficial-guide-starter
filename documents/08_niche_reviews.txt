UCLA student food review - 5 stars
The dining at UCLA is genuinely impressive for a college campus. Bruin Plate changed how I think about campus food. Everything is fresh, locally sourced, and actually tastes good. The variety across all the dining halls means I never get bored.

UCLA student food review - 4 stars
Food is great overall. Bruin Plate is my favorite, the tuna poke bowl is something I look forward to every week. De Neve is more hit or miss but the late night option saves me during finals. Epicuria is solid if you want something lighter.

UCLA student food review - 5 stars
Coming from a school with bad dining I was shocked at how good UCLA food is. The number 1 ranking is deserved. Bruin Plate alone would put them near the top. Add in the other options and it's hard to complain.

UCLA student food review - 3 stars
Dining is good but gets old after a while. By second quarter you've cycled through everything multiple times. The quality is there but the variety across a whole year is limited. Meal plan also feels expensive if you're not eating every meal there.

UCLA student food review - 4 stars
Vegetarian and vegan options at Bruin Plate are the best I've seen at any university. They actually put thought into plant-based meals instead of just offering a salad bar. Huge plus for me.

UCLA student food review - 5 stars
