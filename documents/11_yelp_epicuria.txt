5 stars - Most underrated dining hall at UCLA
Nobody talks about Epicuria enough. The pasta bar alone makes it worth visiting. You pick your pasta and sauce and they make it fresh. The pizza is also legitimately good not just campus food good. Mediterranean options like roasted chicken and grain salads are excellent. Lines are almost always shorter than De Neve which is a huge bonus.

4 stars - Great food, nice atmosphere
Epicuria feels a little more upscale than the other dining halls. The Mediterranean focus means lighter, fresher food which is a nice change from the heavy American options at De Neve. The dining room is nicer and less chaotic. My go-to when I want a more relaxed meal.

4 stars - Best pasta on campus
The fresh pasta bar at Epicuria is something special for a college dining hall. You can customize your order and the pasta is actually cooked properly not overdone. The sauces rotate and are well made. If you have not tried Epicuria yet you are missing out.

3 stars - Good but limited variety
The food quality at Epicuria is high but the menu variety is more limited than De Neve. If you are in the mood for Mediterranean it is great. If you want something different you might leave unsatisfied. I rotate between Epicuria and Bruin Plate depending on what I feel like.

5 stars - Go here to avoid De Neve lines
Seriously. If the De Neve line is wrapping around the building just walk to Epicuria. The food is better in my opinion and the wait is maybe 5 minutes. The Mediterranean food is fresh and flavorful and the pasta bar is a highlight. I switched from De Neve to Epicuria as my main dining hall and never looked back.

4 stars - Perfect for a lighter meal
When I want something healthy but not as strict as Bruin Plate, Epicuria is perfect. The Mediterranean dishes are light and satisfying. Fresh ingredients, simple preparation. The olive oil and herb flavors are a nice break from typical dining hall food.

4 stars - Hidden gem dining hall
More people need to know about Epicuria. Consistently good food, shorter lines, nicer space. The pasta bar changes enough that it stays interesting. Would be my top choice if it had the variety of De Neve but for quality and experience it wins.
